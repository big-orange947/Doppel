# Observation-boundary repair and development regressions

The two opened natural high-config questions are development regressions, not
new independent samples, a full LongMemEval score, or an AML result. Their previous
receipts, answers, flags and scores remain immutable. Do not pool protocols.

## Generic production change

Apply `created_at <= plan.now` to the personal-memory coarse Store/index filter
and authoritative structural qualification, including graph-path revalidation.
This is an observation-clock bound, not a valid/event-time bound. In particular,
do not cap observations at `as_of`: a fact learned now about an earlier period may
answer a historical question. A known future plan remains eligible for an explicit
planned/unbounded lookup. `updated_at` is not an observation cutoff: a later
administrative mutation alone must not hide an otherwise eligible record.

No question keyword, reference answer, annotated position, relation domain or
benchmark ID enters this rule. Synthetic regressions cover ordinary/current/
historical/interval/count queries, the inclusive boundary, late observations of
old facts, future plans, filter-ignoring stores/vector/relations and stale graph
exploration. This is not a historical version-snapshot implementation: merged
records whose earlier `created_at` masks later-added evidence still need separate
version/provenance semantics. Do not claim complete bitemporal reconstruction.
